This paper searches for a **universal truthfulness hyperplane inside large language models**, a direction in the representation space that appears to separate truthful from untruthful content. Junteng Liu is the first author of this work, which was published at EMNLP 2024. Code for this project (Universal_Truthfulness_Hyperplane) is publicly available on GitHub.
