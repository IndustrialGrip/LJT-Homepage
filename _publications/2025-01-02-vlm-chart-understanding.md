This paper investigates the **perception bottleneck of vision-language models (VLMs) for chart understanding** and shows that the perception of chart details, rather than reasoning alone, limits chart comprehension. Junteng Liu is the first author of this work. Code for this project (Vision4Chart) is publicly available on GitHub.
